About me

I design complex systems
that simplify everyday decisions.

My work focuses on fintech, commerce ecosystems, growth experiences, and scalable consumer platforms across Southeast Asia — designing for the nuances of diverse markets while building systems robust enough to scale.

Over the years, I've led initiatives spanning homepage ecosystems, payments infrastructure, collaborative ordering experiences, and platform-level design systems — always with an eye toward long-term scalability and meaningful user outcomes.

I enjoy solving high-ambiguity product challenges that require balancing user needs, business goals, technical constraints, and cross-functional stakeholder dynamics. The best design decisions I've made happened in the intersection of all four.

Before Grab, I designed for Shopee's marketplace and DBS Bank's digital banking platforms — experiences that shaped my approach to designing at scale with empathy.
