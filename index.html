About me

I design product systems
that move users and metrics.

I'm a Lead Product Designer with 12+ years of experience turning ambiguous, high-stakes product problems into scalable systems across fintech, commerce, and ecosystem platforms.

At Grab, I lead design across homepage discovery, payments infrastructure, collaborative ordering, and supermarket commerce — balancing user intent, business growth, technical constraints, and operational scale for 30M+ users across Southeast Asia.

My edge is connecting strategy to craft: defining the product direction, shaping the system architecture, validating through research and metrics, and partnering deeply with PM, engineering, data, marketing, and business teams to ship durable outcomes.

I also work close to production — using AI coding assistants, prototyping tools, and front-end understanding to explore ideas faster, pressure-test interaction details, and collaborate more effectively with engineering.

Previously at Carousell, Shopee, and MooVita, I built deep expertise in marketplace commerce, seller operations, mobility UX, and design systems for multi-market platforms.
